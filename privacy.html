Privacy Policy

ShapeScan · Effective November 25, 2026

ShapeScan does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Photos you capture or open, their settings and the DXF files you export are saved only on your device (and wherever you choose to save or share them). They are never sent to us.

Camera, Bluetooth and local network

ShapeScan uses the camera only to photograph parts when you choose to. It uses Bluetooth and your local network only to find and control your own ShapeScan Raspberry Pi camera: switching its light, setting up its Wi-Fi, and receiving photos from it. This traffic stays between your device and your camera.

Raspberry Pi login

When you use Set Up Camera, the username and password you enter are used only to log in to your Raspberry Pi over SSH. If you choose to remember the password, it is kept in your device's Keychain.

Purchases

ShapeScan Pro is handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
