ShapeScan for Mac, iPhone and iPad

Lay a part on a backlit panel, take a photo, and ShapeScan traces its outline at true size, with an adjustable offset, ready for AutoCAD, Fusion, laser cutters and CNC.

How it works

  • Capture with a Raspberry Pi camera over the light box, or with your iPhone or iPad camera.
  • Measure: the panel's known size sets the scale; lens and perspective are corrected automatically.
  • Trace: outlines become clean lines and splines; holes are found too.
  • Offset the outline outward or inward for clearances and cutter paths.

Free and Pro

Capturing, tracing and measuring are free. ShapeScan Pro, a one-time in-app purchase that covers Mac, iPhone and iPad, adds DXF export and the complete build guide for the ShapeScan light box: printable 3D files, step-by-step assembly, wiring diagram and Raspberry Pi setup.
