Getting started without hardware

Choose Try a Sample Photo (or File → Open Sample Photo on the Mac) to see a traced outline straight away. You can also open any photo of a part on a backlit panel, or use your iPhone or iPad camera.

Accurate measurements

  • Enter the size of the white area inside the black border of your panel.
  • Hold or mount the camera straight above the centre of the panel, with the whole panel in view.
  • Keep the panel clean: dust and scratches show up as edges.

Raspberry Pi camera

  • Install Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit) with Raspberry Pi Imager on a computer, with SSH enabled and your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi set.
  • In ShapeScan, choose Set Up Camera and enter the Pi's address and login. The app installs everything else.
  • No Wi-Fi? ShapeScan finds the Pi over Bluetooth and can switch it to its own hotspot.
  • ShapeScan Pro includes the full illustrated setup and build guide.
